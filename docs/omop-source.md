# OMOP as a source

A hospital whose anaesthesia record already stands in an OMOP database binds the same role contract to those tables, and every question runs there unchanged. The hospital schema is then a map from the contract's parts to OMOP tables rather than to a vendor's, and nothing else of Schemalyser changes. This page says which conventions such an OMOP must meet before the contract parts can be bound to it, so that the binding is a day's work with the page rather than a conversion in its own right, and it names the test by which the binding is judged.

## The conventions the three contract parts need

- **One visit detail for each anaesthetic.** The anaesthetic is the contract's unit, and `role_anaesthetic` needs a row of its own with a start and a stop. An OMOP that records an anaesthetic as a `visit_detail` row of a type that marks it as an anaesthetic, with `visit_detail_start_datetime` and `visit_detail_end_datetime`, binds directly. An OMOP that records only the operation, or only the admission, cannot supply the part, because neither is the anaesthetic.
- **Test patients kept and marked.** `role_patient.is_test` is a column the contract requires, and a question leaves test patients out by it. An OMOP that drops test patients before loading can bind the column as always zero, and says so in the schema's notes; one that keeps them must hold the mark somewhere the schema can read, such as an observation of a fixed concept, since the person table has no column for it.
- **Measurements linked to the visit detail.** `role_reading` belongs to its anaesthetic by key, never by time, so each measurement row must carry `visit_detail_id` of the anaesthetic in which it was charted. An OMOP that links measurements only to the visit occurrence cannot say which of two anaesthetics in one admission a reading belongs to, and the binding stops at the readings.
- **The readings' kinds as concepts.** `role_reading.kind` is a closed vocabulary. The schema translates the measurement concepts into kinds as it translates a vendor's codes, through the page's step for codes; the mean arterial pressure by cuff and by arterial line must be distinguishable, by concept or by a modifier, or the kind cannot be told.
- **Values as recorded.** A reading is each value the monitor recorded, with its own key and time, and whether it was accepted. An OMOP that keeps only hourly or median values cannot supply the readings at the grain the contract has, and a question over minutes below a threshold does not run on it.

The further parts bind the same way where the OMOP holds them at the record's grain: drug exposures linked to the visit detail for the drugs, device exposures for the devices, measurements of fluids for the fluids with their running totals kept apart from amounts, and the visit occurrence for the stay. Where the OMOP holds a derived interval instead of the events, the part is not supplied, as the contract's rule on grain has it.

## The test that judges a binding

The roadmap's step 8 is a round trip, and it should be named as one. The testbed's invented world is converted to OMOP by the conversion steps; a hospital schema is then bound to that OMOP as a source; the transfusion question is compiled through it and through the stand-in's schema; and the two answers must agree, as the equivalence tool compares answers. Done means the round trip agrees, and that the full testbed passes with the steps over the roles in place of the direct ones. It starts when a second site or a research need asks for it, and nothing here is sized until then.
